The2io is Ilya's space for sound, atmosphere and visual storytelling.

Ilya Onchev is a music producer and independent artist working as The2io. His catalogue moves between electronic music, hip-hop-rooted ideas and softer, nostalgic textures, from No Matter How I Try and Faded Horizons to Soft As Cotton Candy and Hit the lyrics.

His music also extends into visual work. Ilya has created original music for CGI showcases, including the SUDANIM project, and later collaborated on an Unreal Engine music video for Soft As Cotton Candy.
